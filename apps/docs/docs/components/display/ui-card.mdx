# Card

A surface that groups content with shared spacing and corners. Choose outlined, subtle or elevated; standard or expressive treatment independently follows the theme's material. Tones change colour without changing geometry.

The standard treatment keeps the card restrained, including the subtle lift of elevated cards. Set `treatment="expressive"` to use the theme's decorative material. Bookish can express it with a frosted frame; matte themes remain opaque. Content stays readable, with reduced-transparency and opaque fallbacks.

Colored tones use a quiet surface tint without changing corner geometry or adding accent stripes. Hovered and selected rows inside use a deeper shade of that surface in light themes, or a lighter shade in dark themes. Neutral nested panels restore brand highlights; action buttons keep their own intent colors.
