# Card

A surface that groups content with shared spacing, corners and material. Choose outlined, subtle, elevated or glass; content stays readable as the surrounding material changes.

The glass variant uses shared glassiness and surface separation for its decorative frame, with an opaque content plane and reduced-transparency fallback.

Colored tones use a quiet surface tint. Hovered and selected rows inside use a deeper shade of that surface in light themes, or a lighter shade in dark themes. Neutral nested panels restore brand highlights; action buttons keep their own intent colors.
