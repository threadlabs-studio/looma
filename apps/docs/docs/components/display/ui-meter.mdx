# Meter

How far along something is, at a glance: the share of an invoice collected, of items reviewed, of a checklist done. `value` runs from 0 to `max` (1 by default, as on a native `<meter>`); a value outside that range draws at the nearer end, so an empty meter still shows its track and a full one fills it. The bar is drawn as the page renders, on the server and before JavaScript.

A meter is `role="meter"` with its value, bounds, and `valueText` as ARIA values; name it with `label`, or with `aria-labelledby` from visible text. `tone` colours the fill to carry a state, such as nothing yet, partial, complete, or needs attention; say that state in `valueText` or in text beside the meter too, since colour alone reaches no one using assistive technology or forced colours. `size="sm"` is a thin bar for a table cell or a list row. Set `--ui-meter-inline-size` on a meter to change its width.

For a count of steps, such as an order moving from placed to delivered, `segments` draws the bar as that many equal segments (from 2 to 12), so it reads as "step 4 of 6" rather than as a percentage. Set `max` to the same number and `value` to the steps done, and say the step in `valueText` ("Shipped, step 4 of 6"), since the segments are drawn, not read. The segments are drawn as the page renders too, and each keeps its own outline in forced colours.
